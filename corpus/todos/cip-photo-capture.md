# Fill a book from a photo of its CIP block

Romanian books print a "Descrierea CIP a Bibliotecii Naționale a României" block
on the copyright page with title, author, publisher, year and ISBN. Photograph
it and have OCR or Claude fill the book form.

## Context

The best fallback for the Romanian books the databases miss; see [metadata-
lookup.md](../wiki/metadata-lookup.md).
