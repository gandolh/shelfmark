# Add a book by ISBN or by hand

One field that accepts a typed ISBN-10 or ISBN-13 (hyphens ignored, check digit
validated) and a USB or Bluetooth scanner (digits then Enter), then runs the
same lookup and confirm card as the camera. When nothing is found, or the book
has no barcode, a fast manual form opens with whatever is known filled in.

## Context

Many Romanian books will need the manual form; see [metadata-
lookup.md](../wiki/metadata-lookup.md). Keyboard-wedge notes in
[scanning.md](../wiki/scanning.md).
