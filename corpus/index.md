## Wiki

- [wiki/architecture.md](wiki/architecture.md) - How Shelfmark is put together, as built by briefs 01-14 on 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15. Three npm workspaces copied from Satchel's layout (shared, server, web), what each owns, the request paths, where data lives, phone testing over HTTPS in dev, and the deploy shape on the shared VPS.
- [wiki/data-model.md](wiki/data-model.md) - The SQLite schema as shipped by brief 02 on 2026-10-14. One row per physical copy (decided) with bibliographic fields, personal fields and provenance, wishlist rows in the same table; tags (matched case-insensitively), covers (as BLOBs) and the planned lookup cache as their own tables; no isbn10 column; defaults, CHECKs and what null means; how ISBNs, authors, author sort, money and dates are stored; and why there is no full-text index.
- [wiki/decisions.md](wiki/decisions.md) - Locked calls for Shelfmark, each with date, rejected alternatives and reason. Physical books only (ebooks stay in Atrium); a web app with a PWA on React, Fastify and SQLite; one user, deployed on the VPS with its own login and Sign in with Ward; English interface; the familiar table app in the Covers variant, light; two places only (Library, Add a book); one row per physical copy; no loans; Google Books needs the owner's free API key; covers live in SQLite; Romanian books are looked up in three library catalogs and the CIP lists before Google, politely; the camera reads only on a press. Research recommendations live on the concept pages until the owner confirms them.
- [wiki/design.md](wiki/design.md) - How Shelfmark's look was chosen and why (owner, 2026-10-14). The familiar table app played straight, in the Covers variant (calibrated on Apple's own apps; the cover leads, chrome recedes, one teal accent, light). The original token table, the two-place navigation (Library, Add a book) with an account menu instead of Settings, the library table and covers grid, the Add a book flow and its three scan outcomes, the mock-ups, and the 2026-10-15 finish review. The system as built is DESIGN.md at the repo root. Read before any UI brief.
- [wiki/export.md](wiki/export.md) - How CSV and Excel export work and the traps they avoid. What gets exported (the current view or everything), the UTF-8 BOM that makes Excel show ș and ț, spreadsheet formula injection, why write-excel-file and not SheetJS, and the full backup (one `.db` file).
- [wiki/features.md](wiki/features.md) - The researched feature set, in tiers (v1, v1.x, later, out; loans are out, the wishlist is v1.x), with what comparable home-library apps (Libib, BookBuddy, CLZ Books, LibraryThing) offer and why each feature sits in its tier. Read before writing or promoting any brief; the todo files carry these tiers as tags.
- [wiki/glossary.md](wiki/glossary.md) - Shelfmark's vocabulary, one word per concept with the synonyms it replaces. Book, collection (owned, wishlist), shelf and room, status, lookup and draft, CIP, library catalog and CIP index. Use these words in code, UI copy and briefs.
- [wiki/library-table.md](wiki/library-table.md) - How the main table of books works. TanStack Table running in the browser over every book, the filter type each column gets, diacritics-insensitive search (Cartarescu finds Cărtărescu, ş and ș match), remembered column layout, virtual scrolling, row selection, and why filtering does not need the server.
- [wiki/metadata-lookup.md](wiki/metadata-lookup.md) - Where a scanned book's title, authors, cover and other details come from. Open Library and Google Books (with the owner's free key) compared, their limits, the hit rates measured on 2026-10-14 (Romanian ISBNs found 12-20% of the time vs 92% for English; every free keyless catalog worse still), why the national library's CIP catalog can't be used, three Romanian library catalogs over Z39.50 measured on 2026-10-17 (33/40 for 2012-2015 books, a year's lag for new ones), the national bibliography PDFs, the lookup flow and cache as shipped by brief 05 (two Open Library calls per found ISBN), how covers are downloaded (the host allowlist, Open Library's archive.org redirects) and stored, the measurement script, and the fallbacks for Romanian books.
- [wiki/open-questions.md](wiki/open-questions.md) - Genuinely unresolved questions. As of 2026-10-14 there are none; the last one (an existing list to import) was answered no. Add a question here the moment one opens, delete it the moment it is answered.
- [wiki/overview.md](wiki/overview.md) - What Shelfmark is in a paragraph. A catalog of the owner's physical books, filled by scanning barcodes with a phone, shown as a sortable, filterable table that exports to CSV and Excel. Who uses it, what it deliberately leaves out, and how it relates to Atrium and Satchel.
- [wiki/scanning.md](wiki/scanning.md) - How the app reads a book's barcode. Browser support for BarcodeDetector (Chrome only by default), the barcode-detector polyfill on ZXing-C++ WASM and why its .wasm is self-hosted, the HTTPS rule, the iOS standalone-PWA camera bug that forces a single-screen scanner, why the picture looks soft (the stream's size, iPhone's lens switching, what Chrome on Android lets a page set) and what we ask for since brief 21 (1080p, continuous focus, a 1×/2× zoom chip, no lens picker), the `?camera` readout, scanning on a press (read until a code is read, *Stop reading*, a minute's limit, a sticker's 1 s wait, the button only while no result is open), ISBN-10/13 validation, non-ISBN barcodes, and USB keyboard-wedge scanners.
- [wiki/status.md](wiki/status.md) - Snapshot as of 2026-10-15. v1 is built - all 14 briefs done, reviewed and committed on main (515 tests), pushed, not deployed. Briefs 15-16 (Romanian library catalogs, the CIP index) 17-20 (one lookup per ISBN, scan on a press, the first read wins) written and built 2026-10-17/18 (618 tests), not deployed. Brief 21 (a press reads until it finds the ISBN; a sharper camera and a camera readout) written 2026-10-20, built 2026-10-21 (633 tests), not deployed. What waits on the owner (Google key, deploy, phone and Ward checks), one line per brief, and the todos left by tier.
