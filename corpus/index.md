One row per physical copy (decided) with bibliographic fields, personal fields and provenance, wishlist rows in the same table; tags (matched case-insensitively), covers (as BLOBs), the lookup cache and saved views (migration 3, unique names via name_key) as their own tables; bulk changes in one transaction; no isbn10 column; defaults, CHECKs and what null means; how ISBNs, authors, author sort, money and dates are stored; and why there is no full-text index.
- [wiki/decisions.md](wiki/decisions.md) - Locked calls for Shelfmark, each with date, rejected alternatives and reason. Research recommendations live on the concept pages until the owner confirms them; only lightweight tests (nothing boots the app).

- [wiki/metadata-lookup.md](wiki/metadata-lookup.md) - Where a scanned book's title, authors, cover and other details come from. - [wiki/overlays.md](wiki/overlays.md) - How Shelfmark's menus, panels, dialogs, tabs and toggles sit on Base UI since 2026-10-23. - [wiki/overview.md](wiki/overview.md) - What Shelfmark is in a paragraph. A catalog of the owner's physical books, filled by scanning barcodes with a phone, shown as a sortable, filterable table that exports to CSV and Excel. Who uses it, what it deliberately leaves out, and how it relates to Atrium and Satchel.
